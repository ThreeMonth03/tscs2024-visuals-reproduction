## Survey materials and aggregate results

The study uses the 2024 Taiwan Social Change Survey, Cycle 8, Year 5, Digital Society module. Obtain official survey materials from [TSCS](https://www2.ios.sinica.edu.tw/sc/cht/scDownload2.php#eighth) or [SRDA](https://srda.sinica.edu.tw/plan/?idx=SRDA.AS002), and follow the provider's access, use, and acknowledgement requirements. See the [official usage rules](https://www2.ios.sinica.edu.tw/sc/cht/scDownload3.php).

This package supplies study-produced aggregate results for checking the reported analyses and regenerating figures. Cite the study and the survey source when using these results. Their inclusion does not grant a new license over the underlying questionnaire or respondent data. No CC BY license for those survey materials is asserted here.

No respondent records or questionnaire reproductions are distributed. Users obtain official data themselves and may run the local survey workflow under the provider’s terms. Upload to a cloud session only when the data-use conditions permit that processing; the repository and its software license grant no additional data rights. Public CI uses artificial inputs and existing aggregates only. Input files, profiles and metadata catalogues are excluded from result downloads.
